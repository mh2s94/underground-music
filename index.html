    Manifest

    UNDERGROUND is an independent publication for the heavy end of music: the bands, records and scenes shaping metalcore, hardcore, deathcore and everything louder.

    No label money, no pay-to-play reviews. We cover what moves us, from arena-sized breakdowns to demos recorded in a bedroom, and we write about it honestly.
