Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026 · added visitor statistics

UNDERGROUND (music.mikasa.me) is an independent music magazine run by Mauliyadi Hamsa in Indonesia. This page explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep it to the minimum the site needs.

What we collect

  • Reading the site: no account needed. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell data.
  • Member account: your email address, a password (stored only as a secure hash), your display name and the favourite genres you choose.
  • What members do on the site: saved articles, comments you post, and star ratings you give to songs.
  • Newsletter: if you subscribe, your email address and where you signed up. Every newsletter has a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • Demo submissions: band name, country, genre, contact email, the link and message you send us.
  • Technical data: our hosting providers process your IP address and browser details to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
  • Visitor statistics: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits, pages, countries, device types and page speed without cookies and without identifying or following individual people. The footer shows a summary of these statistics: the share of page views per country, as percentages only.

Why we use it

  • To run your account, sign you in, and show your saved articles and ratings.
  • To publish your comments and count your ratings in the public charts (charts show totals, never who voted).
  • To listen to and reply to demo submissions.
  • To keep the site secure, for example limiting how often forms can be sent.

Who processes it

We use trusted services to run the site. They process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: accounts, sign-in and the database.
  • Cloudflare: hosting, music file storage, security and the AI tools editors use to draft articles.
  • Resend: delivers the newsletter to subscribers.
  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr: fonts and code libraries loaded by your browser.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode), Deezer and Apple iTunes: embedded videos, 30-second song previews and cover artwork. Playing them loads content from those services.

Some of these providers store data outside Indonesia. They protect it under their own security and privacy commitments.

Storage on your device

Your browser keeps your sign-in session and small preferences in local storage so you stay signed in. Clearing your browser data signs you out.

How long we keep it

Account data stays until you ask us to delete your account. Demo submissions are kept for up to 12 months. Comments stay published until you or an editor remove them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent, at any time, in line with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU No. 27 Tahun 2022). You can change your display name and genres, or delete your account and everything linked to it, yourself on the Account page. For anything else, email us and we will act within 14 days.

Contact: hello@mikasa.me

Children

Accounts are for people aged 13 and over. Some music and artwork covered here is intended for mature audiences.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Important changes will be announced on the site.
